About me

The part I like best.

I've spent eleven years in tech, and the part I like best is the stretch between "we signed the customer" and "we're live." I started in IT support at Cognizant and worked through MathWorks, Amazon and BirdEye, and that background taught me how to stay calm when something breaks and how to explain it to people who don't speak tech.

Today I lead healthcare SaaS implementations at Steer Health. I work with Product, Engineering, Customer Success and our clients to turn requirements into a plan, test everything properly with UAT, and get the product launched and stable. Lately that has included EHR integrations and checking that our AI-powered patient conversations are ready before they reach a client.

I'm sitting my PMP exam in November and have finished PMI-ACP training, but what I care about most is keeping everyone clear on what's next and who's doing it.
